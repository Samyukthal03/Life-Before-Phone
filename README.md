Life Before Phone

A lightweight, single-page web app celebrating simple, screen-free childhood memories and pastimes.

Summary
This project is an interactive digital museum featuring 12 nostalgic activities we used to do before smartphones existed. Everything runs directly in the browser with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript—no extra setups, installations, or frameworks required.

Included Activities
1. Sand food: Shape sand in a pot, add water, and garnish with leaves or flowers.
2. Water tub swirl: Stir the water in circles to build a mini whirlpool.
3. Raindrops on the window: Tap falling drops to speed them up and race them down.
4. Kicking stones: Tap the stone to kick and bounce it along the path.
5. Watching ants: Drop a breadcrumb on the ground and watch ants track it down.
6. DVD hitting the corner: Watch the logo bounce around the screen and nudge it toward a corner hit.
7. Pambaram: Pull and release the cord to spin a traditional top.
8. Eraser pencil dots: Poke pencil holes and graphite marks into a classic eraser.
9. Two cylinder magnets: Move two magnets together to see them attract or push apart.
10. Chips on fingers: Place cone chips onto your fingers like claws.
11. Water bottle tornado: Swirl the bottle in circles to form a water vortex.
12. Peel the gum: Pour glue onto your palm, let it dry, and carefully peel it off.

How to Run
1. Download or open index.html.
2. Open it in any web browser.
3. Pick any card from the grid to play.
